# **App Name**: Satish ChillTech

## Core Features:

- Dynamic Service Hub: Comprehensive landing sections showcasing sales and spares for AC, refrigeration, washing machines, RO systems, and UPS systems.
- PCB Service Expert Page: A dedicated detailed page highlighting specialized airconditioning PCB repair services as specified in the business portfolio.
- Unified Enquiry Tool: Smart contact forms that allow users to select specific service categories (repair, product purchase, or spares) for targeted business leads.
- Brand Assurance Gallery: A professional showcase of supported brands including Samsung, LG, Whirlpool, Hitachi, and more to establish technical authority.
- Kanchipuram Operations Portal: Integration of location data and contact protocols for the North Street branch, facilitating easy local access.
- Regulatory & Trust Pages: Mandatory Privacy Policy and Terms of Service pages ensuring professional compliance.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary Color: Electric Cyan (#00e0ff), representing modern cooling technology and high-tech precision.
- Secondary Color: Dark Charcoal (#353838), used for text and structural elements to provide a professional, grounded feel.
- Background Color: Pure White (#ffffff), ensuring a clean, clinical service environment that prioritizes content clarity.
- Headline Font: 'Space Grotesk' for a modern, mechanical, and technical aesthetic. Body Font: 'Inter' for high legibility and an objective look.
- Thin-line technical icons representing various cooling hardware and electronic components like PCBs.
- A modular, grid-based layout reminiscent of professional appliance service manuals, utilizing generous whitespace and clear sectioning.
- Micro-transitions like 'cool' fades and sliding entry effects for card components as users scroll through service details.
